# How D2C Brands Can Get Ads Made Without the Freelancer Hassle

Ask a D2C founder what slows down their video ads and you rarely hear "ideas". You hear about freelancers.

Not because freelancers are bad. Many are brilliant. The problem is everything around the work: finding the right one, explaining what you want, waiting for a quote, chasing the draft, and doing it all again for the next ad.

Here is where the hassle actually comes from, and how to remove it.

## Hassle 1: Finding someone who fits

Post a requirement and you get fifty portfolios. Most are wedding films, music videos or personal reels. Very few show an ad a brand in your category actually paid for.

Sorting through them takes days, and you still don't know who can deliver a skincare ad that converts, as opposed to a pretty video.

**On Dalaillama:** you describe the video, and the request is routed to an active creator who makes videos for brands in your industry. Or you pick one yourself from their profile. Every film on a creator's profile was made on the platform and delivered to a client who paid for it, so the portfolio is proof of delivered work, not a showreel.

## Hassle 2: The price comes last

With freelancers, the price usually arrives after a call, two rounds of messages and a "depends on the requirements". You can't plan a campaign around a number you don't have.

**On Dalaillama:** your request comes back as a brief with the price on it, usually the same day. A 60-second video starts from ₹1,999 in India ($20 elsewhere), and the rest is the creator's own rate for the work. You see the number before you commit to anything.

## Hassle 3: The brief gets lost

The script is in a Google Doc, the references are in WhatsApp, the feedback is in an email thread, and the "final_v3_FINAL" file is in someone's Drive. Every revision means re-explaining.

**On Dalaillama:** the brief, the storyboard review and the final video live in one link. Feedback attaches to the thing it's about.

## Hassle 4: You see the work too late

The classic freelance failure: you wait a week, get a finished video, and it's wrong in a way that would have been obvious from a sketch. Now every fix is a re-edit.

**On Dalaillama:** you approve the storyboard before the film is made, so a wrong direction is caught when it costs nothing to change. Then you review the finished ad, with captions, voice and sound included.

## Hassle 5: Paying a stranger upfront, or being asked to

Full payment upfront is a risk for you. No payment upfront is a risk for the freelancer. Either way, someone is uncomfortable, and that slows everything.

**On Dalaillama:** you pay a share upfront to start, and the rest on approval of the final video. Both sides know where they stand.

## Hassle 6: Starting over for every ad

Even when a freelancer works out, the next ad means checking their availability, re-sharing the brand guidelines, and hoping they remember the tone. If they're busy, you're back to hassle 1.

**On Dalaillama:** the creator who made your last ad already has your brand context, and you can request the next one from their profile. If they're unavailable, the request is routed to another creator in your industry.

## When a freelancer is still the right choice

If you need a live shoot with a crew, a long-term in-house editor, or a creative partner embedded in your team, hire for that. Dalaillama is built for the steady stream of ads, variations and launch videos most D2C brands need every month, made by creators who work in AI-assisted production.
