# How Dalaillama Takes the Selling Out of Being a Creator

Ask any video creator what's hardest about the work, and it's rarely the work.

It's selling. Finding brands that might need a video. Working out who to email. Writing a pitch that doesn't sound like spam. Following up. Sending a portfolio link that shows the wrong videos. Hearing nothing back. Doing it again next week, while also trying to make the videos that pay the bills.

Most creators spend more hours looking for clients than making films for them. Dalaillama is built to flip that. Here's how the sales process works on the platform, step by step, and what it costs you: nothing to start.

## Step 1: Create your profile, free

Sign up at [creator.dalaillama.in](https://creator.dalaillama.in) and you get a public creator profile on the Free plan. It's a page brands can find, follow and request videos from. No card, no trial that runs out.

Pick the industries you make videos for (food, skincare, fashion, fitness, and so on). That one choice decides which brands see your work and which requests come to you, so be specific.

## Step 2: Link your YouTube channel

Most creators already have their best work on YouTube. There's no need to re-upload it.

Link your channel and prove it's yours, then choose which videos go on your profile. They play in the YouTube player, with YouTube's attribution, right on your page. Your portfolio is built from the work you've already made, in minutes.

Films you make in the Dalaillama studio for paying clients join your profile too, marked as delivered client work. That's the strongest proof a brand can see: not "I can make videos", but "a brand like yours paid for this one".

## Step 3: We find brands that may like your work

This is where the selling stops being your job.

Dalaillama keeps adding brands, and the right person to contact at each, to its outreach audience, organised by industry. The list grows every week, so the number of brands your work can reach keeps growing without you lifting a finger.

From that audience, brands in your industry are sent a film they haven't seen yet, chosen from creators who make videos for that industry. Yours included.

You don't write anything, choose anyone, or press send. It runs on its own, and it doesn't use up your own email allowance. On the Free plan, your work is shown this way to a few brands, up to 10 a month. Studio raises that to 100 a month, and Pro to every brand in the audience.

And when a brand follows you, they hear about every new film you publish. Your audience grows instead of resetting with every pitch.

## Step 4: Reach out yourself, with templates that don't read like spam

If you also want to pitch brands directly, you don't need to write a cold email from scratch. Pick a ready template, choose a film from your profile, and add a personal note if you like. Emails go out from your own Dalaillama address, under your name.

Four templates are ready to use:

- **Share a film:** for showing one strong piece of work.
- **Made for a brand like yours:** for showing work you did for a brand in the same industry.
- **My recent work:** a short portfolio of your latest films.
- **Video ideas for your brand:** a few videos brands in their industry are running, offered as ideas you could make for them.

Here's roughly how "Made for a brand like yours" reads when it reaches a skincare brand (the subject and opening line are the template's own; the names are examples):

**Subject:** A film I made for a skincare brand

Hi Priya,

I recently made this film for a brand in skincare, and thought it might be useful for yours.

[The film plays here, with a link to watch it on my profile.]

If you'd like something like it for your products, you can request a video from my profile in one click. You'll get a brief with the price, and nothing is charged until you approve it.

Asha Rao
Video creator on Dalai Llama

The platform fills in the brand's name, your film and the industry, adds an unsubscribe link, and handles delivery. You only decide what to send and to whom.

## Step 5: One email a day per brand, never spam

The fastest way to burn a brand's attention is to email them three times in a week. So Dalaillama enforces a simple rule for everyone: **a brand receives at most one email a day from the platform**, whoever it's from. Anything more waits for the next day's digest. Brands that unsubscribe are never emailed again.

That protects the brands, and it protects you. Your email arrives in an inbox that isn't flooded, from an address with a clean sending record, so it actually gets read.

## Step 6: Brands request, and the request arrives as a brief

When a brand likes your work, they don't have to find your number, email you, or wait for a reply. They press **Request a video** on your profile, or on the film they saw in their inbox, and tell you what they need: the product, who it's for, the length and the languages.

You get that as a brief. You set the price on it, the brand reviews it, and pays a share upfront to start. Requests from brands in your industry that don't name a creator are routed to active creators who make videos for that industry too.

No sales call, no "can you send your rates", no chasing an invoice before you start.
